Guide to Using the map:

Use the search bar to find restaurants, desserts, and food trucks near you. You can also filter results by cuisine type and price range.

Click on a marker to see more details about a location, including reviews and ratings from other users.

Use the "Locate Me" button to center the map on your current location and find nearby food options.

Don't forget to check the filters to narrow down your search results!

Have fun exploring the map!

Happy eating!
